% ─── Projects ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Notable Projects}

\noindent\textbf{\color{primary}SGI LAB MANAGER} \hfill
\href{https://github.com/FoxyYTs/SGI_LAB_MANAGER_FRONTEND}{\small\faGithub\ FoxyYTs/SGI\_LAB\_MANAGER\_FRONTEND}

Cross-platform lab management platform (Android, Web, Windows, Linux) built with
Flutter/Dart and Django REST Framework. Own infrastructure: Docker Compose, Nginx,
Gunicorn, PostgreSQL 15, Cloudflare Zero Trust Tunnel, and automated encrypted backups
(GPG AES-256) with Telegram monitoring. Modules: GHS inventory with AI-powered SDS
extraction (Groq/Llama 3.1), loans, weekly scheduling, 6 PDF report types, and public
QR forms. 92 Django tests · 71 pytest e2e · 22 Flutter e2e. v1.0 stable (Jun 2026).

\vspace{6pt}
\noindent\textbf{\color{primary}Interactive Academic Curriculum — PCJIC Rionegro} \hfill
\href{https://github.com/FoxyYTs/pensum-interactivo}{\small\faGithub\ FoxyYTs/pensum-interactivo} \quad
\href{https://foxyyts.github.io/pensum-interactivo}{\small\faGlobe\ Live site}

Interactive curriculum visualizer for all 8 degree programs at the Rionegro campus of
Politécnico Colombiano Jaime Isaza Cadavid. Directed graph with D3.js showing courses,
prerequisites, credits, and completion percentage, with \texttt{localStorage} persistence.
PWA deployed on GitHub Pages with automated CI/CD via GitHub Actions. Adapted fork of a
Chilean open-source project, with full terminology migration and new features.

\vspace{6pt}
\noindent\textbf{\color{primary}Virtual VR Laboratory} \hfill
\textit{\small\color{darkgray}In development — June 2026}

Successor to the Immersive Learning Environments research group. Virtual lab platform
for the Politécnico's Meta Quest 2 headsets, reusing the Bloque R27 spaces digitized
during the research group as the setting for interactive lab sessions. 4 modules
(Physics, Waves \& Optics, Chemistry, Electricity \& Magnetism) with 12 guided
practices plus one VR-exclusive practice per module. Built with Unity 6.3 LTS, C\#,
OpenXR, and Meta XR SDK. Three modes: Meta Quest 2 standalone, PC VR, and desktop.
